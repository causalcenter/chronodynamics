### Phase 0: Initial Signs (June 2016)

- **June 10-16, 2016**: E12 shows first anomalies (15.4%, 15.1% PC2)
- **Interpretation**: Subtle drift, likely thermal or early clock degradation

### Phase 1: Early Warnings (September 2016)

- **Sept 18, 2016**: E11 severe spike (21.9%)
- **Sept 23-24, 2016**: E12 moderate anomalies (18.1%, 18.6%)
- **Interpretation**: Multiple satellites showing instability

### Phase 2: Crisis Origin (November 2016)

- **Nov 28, 2016**: E19 anomaly (18.4%)
- **Nov 29, 2016**: E19 severe (22.9%)
- **Nov 30, 2016 11:05 UTC**: **E11 CRITICAL EVENT (27.2%)** ← **"Day Zero"**

### Phase 4: Ongoing Instability (March-August 2017)

### Phase 5: September 2017 Crisis Peak

- **Sept 13-17, 2017**: E19 sustained anomalies (20.3% → 17.2%)

### Phase 6: Persistent Issues (December 2017)

- **Dec 8-29, 2017**: E19 chronic instabilities (6 events)
- **Dec 16-18, 2017**: E12 and E11 recurrences
